Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about QueueLess.

Is QueueLess free to use?

Yes! This demo version of QueueLess is completely free to use and explore. It runs entirely in your browser with simulated data.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed for this demo. Your queue data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

How accurate is the wait time estimate?

Estimates are based on the average service time of the selected service and the number of people ahead of you. In this demo, they update dynamically as the queue advances.

Can I use QueueLess on my phone?

Absolutely! QueueLess is fully responsive and optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

What happens if I leave the queue?

You can leave the queue at any time from the Active Queue page. Your token will be removed and the queue will adjust accordingly.
